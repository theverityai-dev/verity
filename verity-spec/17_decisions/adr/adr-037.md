# Architectural Decision Record

## ADR-037: A client exports its own data; the operator never exports a client's business rows

* **Decision ID**: ADR-037
* **Status**: ACCEPTED (2026-10-09), decided by the engineering lead under the product owner's
  standing instruction to take open decisions from project context (Task 125 item 7.5).
* **Question**: HQ audit B5 asks for full export and offboarding of a leaving client (India's DPDP
  Act gives the data principal and the fiduciary's customer rights over their data). An operator
  exporting every client row would bypass `enforcePolicy()`, because the operator role holds no
  business grants (before ADR-035) and reads no business tables.
* **Decision**:
  1. **Export is the client's act.** A client administrator with a new `Export` verb on a new
     `verity.platform.data_export` entity requests a full export of their own tenant. It runs as that
     person through `enforcePolicy()`, reads each exportable entity through the client's own RLS, and
     omits whatever that person may not read (so an export never exceeds the requester's authority).
     Output: one ZIP of CSV files per entity plus a manifest (entity, row count, time, requester),
     written to the tenant's own object-storage prefix and downloaded through a signed link.
  2. **The operator's part is process, not data.** Verity operators start offboarding by suspending
     the client (ADR-034), telling the client's administrator to run the export, and recording the
     date. After the agreed retention period the operator marks the client `archived`; deletion of
     the tenant's rows is a documented operator runbook run with the client's written request, not an
     HQ button, and is logged in the platform audit.
  3. **ADR-035 does not change this.** An operator who has entered a client could technically read
     everything the owner can, but a bulk export by an operator is refused as a design: it would put
     a tenant's whole dataset in a Verity operator's hands without the client's own act.
* **What this is not**: not a change to INV-001, ADR-013, ADR-017, ADR-034, ADR-035 or
  `enforcePolicy()`. No new cross-tenant path.
* **Consequences**: a new entity and verb, an export job (it can be long, so it runs through the job
  runner of ADR-015), and a manifest test. **Built 2026-10-09** (first slice): the `Export` verb and
  `verity.platform.data_export` entity (migrations `20261009070000_platform_data_export` and
  `20261009071000_data_export_grants`), `request_data_export` (runs as the requester through the job runner,
  skips every dataset the requester cannot read and says so in the manifest), a ZIP of CSVs plus
  `manifest.csv` and `export-info.csv` stored under the tenant prefix, download links that need Export (not
  only Read) and expire in five minutes, and Settings > Export your data. Datasets register per capability
  with `registerExportable`: guests, loyalty points, menu (cost price deliberately left out), orders, order
  lines and bills so far. **Open:** the remaining capabilities' datasets (finance, inventory, HR, attendance,
  and the rest) register as each is reviewed, because an export that silently includes a field-restricted
  column is worse than a smaller one; archived client status and the offboarding runbook (clause 2) are process,
  not code, and are not written yet; the runner is synchronous with a 100 MB ceiling, so a very large client
  needs the streaming runner a queue would bring.**
