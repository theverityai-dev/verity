# Architectural Decision Record

## ADR-033: Verity's interface follows Apple's iOS Human Interface Guidelines

* **Decision ID**: ADR-033
* **Status**: ACCEPTED (product owner, 2026-10-05)
* **Supersedes**: the visual parts of ADR-012, ADR-023, ADR-024, ADR-025 and ADR-026 listed
  under "What this replaces". ADR-011's accessibility constraints and ADR-012's monochrome mark
  are kept.
* **Question**: What visual and interaction language does every Verity surface use — every
  client workspace and the HQ operator console?
* **Context**: Clients judge Verity against mature software and report that it does not feel
  finished (`docs/audits/2026-10-04-ui-completeness-audit.md`, client complaints of
  2026-10-05). The interface had accumulated a bespoke material system: an atmospheric accent
  field with grain behind every page, gradient-lit translucent cards, an accent-tinted leading
  edge on each card, light-weight display headings and custom semantic colours. The default
  accent had already moved from Gold to Apple system blue `#0A84FF` without an ADR recording it.
  The product owner instructed on 2026-10-05: adapt Verity's UI and UX to Apple iOS, globally,
  for all clients and HQ, "exactly like iOS"; keep the current font. Reference:
  <https://developer.apple.com/design/resources/> and the Human Interface Guidelines.
* **Decision**: Verity's interface is an iOS interface rendered on the web, on every shell.

  **Colour (iOS system colours, accessible variants).**
  * Backgrounds are iOS grouped backgrounds: canvas `systemGroupedBackground`
    (`#F2F2F7` light, `#000000` dark); content cells `secondarySystemGroupedBackground`
    (`#FFFFFF` / `#1C1C1E`); nested `tertiarySystemGroupedBackground` (`#F2F2F7` / `#2C2C2E`).
  * Labels use iOS label colours; secondary and tertiary labels use Apple's
    increased-contrast values so body text keeps WCAG AA (ADR-011 constraint 1 is not traded).
  * Separators are iOS `separator` (`rgb(60 60 67 / .29)` light, `rgb(84 84 88 / .65)` dark).
  * Tint (accent) defaults to iOS `systemBlue` `#0A84FF`, still user-configurable through the
    existing preset mechanism; contrast stays computed in `src/server/platform/accent.ts`.
  * Semantic colours are iOS system colours (green, orange, red, blue, gray) in their
    accessible variants; they stay independent of the tint.

  **Materials.** Translucent material (blur plus saturation, iOS "bar" and "thick" materials) is
  used only where iOS uses it: navigation bars, the tab bar and sidebar, menus, popovers and
  sheets. Content is opaque: cards and panels are inset-grouped cells — solid, 12px corners, no
  border, no shadow. The atmospheric field and grain are removed. No gradients on surfaces.

  **Typography.** The current system font stack is kept (it renders SF Pro on Apple devices
  without embedding the font, which Apple's licence does not allow on the web). Sizes and
  weights follow iOS text styles: Large Title 34/41 bold for page titles; Title 3 / Headline for
  section titles; Body 17 on phones and 15 on wider screens (dense operate surfaces); Footnote 13
  for secondary text and section headers. Inputs are at least 16px on phones so iOS Safari does
  not zoom.

  **Controls.** Buttons follow iOS button styles: filled (tint background), gray (system fill),
  tinted (tint at low opacity) and plain (tint text); 10px corners; semibold labels. Text fields
  are system-fill rounded fields. Toggles, segmented controls and lists follow iOS shapes.

  **Navigation.** Large title in the navigation area; on phones a bottom tab bar replaces the
  sidebar for the primary destinations, and dialogs present as bottom sheets; on wide screens a
  translucent sidebar (iPadOS style).

  **Motion.** iOS spring motion (critically damped default) with reduced-motion cross-fades,
  unchanged from ADR-024's mechanism.

* **What this replaces**: ADR-024/026 glass on cards and panels, the atmospheric field and grain
  (ADR-026), the Gold default accent (ADR-024) and its "large accent fill" rule (kept in spirit:
  iOS uses tint for controls, not large fills), the accent leading-edge highlight on cards,
  ADR-012's brand-sheet neutrals (`#F7F8FA` etc.) and custom semantic hues, and the h1
  32/40 Light typography spec. `design/verity-aesthetics-v2-gold.png` is history.
* **What this keeps**: WCAG AA on both themes (ADR-011 constraint 1); `prefers-reduced-transparency`
  flattens materials to opaque; `prefers-reduced-motion`; the Verity mark is monochrome and never
  tinted (ADR-012); semantic colour never equals the tint; light and dark are one system.
* **Consequences**:
  * Tokens change in one place (`src/app/globals.css`); the class names `.glass-shell`,
    `.glass-card`, `.glass-control`, `.glass-overlay` and `.verity-solid` are kept and redefined
    to their iOS equivalents so every screen changes without per-file edits.
  * Shared primitives (`src/components/ui/primitives.tsx`, `Modal.tsx`, `DataTable.tsx`) and the
    shells (`ShellChrome.tsx`, `HqChrome.tsx`) carry the iOS component shapes.
  * `CLAUDE.md`'s Experience System section and the `verity-design-companion` skill are rewritten
    to this ADR.
