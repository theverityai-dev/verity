# ADR-053: Verity "Obsidian" — frosted-glass identity for every client, dark and light

**Status:** ACCEPTED (product-owner decision, 2026-10-22: direction D, with glass, images, dark and light, "for all clients").
**Supersedes:** the visual rules of ADR-033 (iOS Human Interface Guidelines on every surface): opaque flat cells, no shadow or border,
no gradients, no blur on content, Large-Title type, bold-only figures. It also supersedes the visual parts of ADR-011/012/023/024/026
that ADR-033 had already superseded.
**Keeps:** WCAG AA in both themes (ADR-011 constraint 1), the monochrome Verity mark (ADR-012), the single-seed tint and its AA
pairing (`accent.ts`), semantic colours independent of the tint, 44pt touch targets on phones, `prefers-reduced-motion` and
`prefers-reduced-transparency`, the iOS tab bar and bottom-sheet behaviour on phones, the line-list rules, and the rule that tokens
and shared parts change, not individual pages.

## Why

The product owner compared Verity with refined reference dashboards and asked why it did not look professionally designed. The
causes, measured on 2026-10-22: iOS idioms stretched across a desktop tool; a 13 to 34 px type scale in the system font with
headings tracked out; cells with no border, no shadow and one radius on a pure `#000` or `#f2f2f7` plane; one blue used on small
things only; a loud solid-blue selected row; screens composed as grids of equal cards with no pictures. A flat refined palette
was tried and rejected as "boring". Direction D (Obsidian) was chosen after five directions were compared on one real screen.

## Decision

Verity's colours do not change: black, graphite, the iOS-blue tint, and the green, orange and red used for status. What changes is
the material, the type, the detail and the composition.

- **Material.** Cells are frosted glass: a translucent gradient fill with `backdrop-filter: blur(32px) saturate(1.7)`, a hairline,
  a 1 px inner top highlight, and a gradient specular rim (bright top-left, cool blue bottom-right). Under them sits a light field
  of four large blue radial blooms fixed behind the content, so the blur has something to bend. Bars (sidebar, top bar) are glass;
  overlays are thicker glass. A cell inside a cell is flat (one blur level per stack). `prefers-reduced-transparency` makes every
  material solid. This replaces ADR-033's "materials only on bars and overlays".
- **Type.** Inter, with the display weights the identity board always printed: 200 for page titles and hero figures, 300 for section
  titles, 500 for card titles and controls, 400 for body. Page titles track at `-0.045em`, figures at `-0.06em`, with tabular
  numerals. IBM Plex Mono at 11.5 px, uppercase, tracked `0.06em`, carries micro-labels (stat labels, section labels, timestamps).
  Desktop body is 14 px, phones 16 px (inputs stay at least 16 px). Figures are no longer required to be bold.
- **Graphics.** Real data drawn precisely: ring gauges with a tick scale, 24-tick bars for piece counts, hairline area charts with a
  dashed crosshair, hairline columns instead of boxes. Colour is state, never decoration.
- **Composition.** Screens are composed to their data (`screen-deserves`) and carry pictures where the data has them
  (`screen-imagery`): thumbnails on every entity, covers and banners for featured objects and module headers, evidence photos,
  initials avatars, empty-state art. Every card is one real clickable object, fully visible and properly sized; nothing is clipped,
  scrolls sideways on desktop, or is a meaningless tile.
- **Motion.** One authored entrance per page, exponential ease-out, collapsing under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Light mode** is the same design with the same layout: `#F2F2F7` canvas, white glass at higher opacity over soft blue blooms,
  black text, the accessible blue, and the darker status inks.

### Anti-regression (replaces ADR-033's)

No translucency on individual table rows, badges or status dots (glass is for cells, bars and overlays). No gradient text. No hard
offset shadows. No new per-page colours outside the tokens. No card that is clipped, scrolls sideways on desktop, or has nothing to
open. Never trade accessibility for appearance: text on glass is checked against the composited colour in both themes.

## Consequences

- Every client workspace and HQ changes look at once through `globals.css`, `primitives.tsx`, the shells and `PieceFlow.tsx`.
- ADR-033's anti-regression list on shadows, gradients, light figures and blur no longer applies; its phone behaviour stays.
- The default accent stays systemBlue with its AA-safe pairing (a lighter blue fill with dark ink in dark mode). The mock's brighter
  `#0A84FF` button with white text measures 4.0:1 and is not used for small text.
- Pictures need a file field on the entities that show them (cover on lot, evidence on return, avatar on karigar), through the
  platform file store. Until a client has uploaded them, the typed tile or initials show and the layout does not change.
- Performance: glass uses `backdrop-filter` on cells only, never on rows, and nested glass is disabled.
