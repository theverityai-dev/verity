# Architectural Decision Record

## ADR-028: Liquid-Glass Refraction on the Existing Glass Materials

* **Decision ID**: ADR-028
* **Question**: Should the four ADR-026 glass material kinds gain real
  refraction (edge bending with chromatic fringing), or stay as flat blur plus
  tint?
* **Context**: ADR-026 established shell/card/control/overlay glass as blur
  plus translucent gradient plus edge light. The product owner directed a
  higher-end, more transparent liquid-glass look, applied everywhere glass is
  already legitimate. The `liquid-glass` skill provides a proven technique: a
  canvas-drawn displacement map feeding SVG `feDisplacementMap`, applied
  through `backdrop-filter: url()`.
* **Decision**: Add refraction as an enhancement layer over the existing four
  kinds. It introduces no new surface kind and no opt-in markup: any element
  already wearing `.glass-shell`, `.glass-card`, `.glass-control` or
  `.glass-overlay` is upgraded by `src/components/ui/LiquidGlass.tsx`, mounted
  once in the root layout. The upgraded state is marked with `.lg-on`, which
  thins shell and card tint and adds a pointer-tracked specular rim.
* **Constraints** (all carry ADR-011/024/026 forward unchanged):
  1. Chromium only. Elsewhere nothing is touched and the frosted blur remains.
  2. `prefers-reduced-transparency` disables it, including mid-session.
     `prefers-reduced-motion` disables the pointer-tracked rim.
  3. Only on-screen surfaces hold a live filter; hard cap of 24 at once; the
     map is drawn at half resolution and cached by size.
  4. Overlay and control keep their existing tint. Dialog and control text keep
     the AA contrast floor; refraction never justifies lowering tint there.
  5. Still never applied to rows, badges or status dots (they wear no glass
     class, so they cannot be upgraded). `.verity-solid` surfaces are unaffected.
  6. The rim is a `::before`; a statically positioned surface is promoted to
     `position: relative`, fixed/absolute/sticky surfaces are left alone.
* **Status**: `ACCEPTED`
* **Owner**: Product Owner
* **Supersedes**: nothing. Extends ADR-026 (material) and ADR-011 constraint 2
  (capped blur layers; the cap is now also a filter cap).
* **Implementation**: `src/components/ui/LiquidGlass.tsx`, mounted in
  `src/app/layout.tsx`; `.lg-on` rules in `src/app/globals.css`.
* **Verification**: Verified in Chromium against the live shell and the
  Prospects page (dark theme): refraction engaged on shell, cards and controls
  with legible text. Light theme and non-Chromium fallback not yet checked.
