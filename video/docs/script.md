# Verity for Manufacturing: video script

Running time 4:26.6 (1920×1080, 60 fps). Times are from the start of the film. Narration is the voice-over; on-screen text is the large statement shown on the left; the description says what the picture does while the line is spoken. All company, people and figures are fictional.

**On screen:** Caption: "Booster pump skid · 2 × 75 kW / Aldervane Fluid Systems". A technical drawing of the skid draws itself in warm line on a dark background.

**[0:03.2] Narration:** A manufacturing business can have skilled engineers, good machines, and suppliers it trusts.

**[0:09.7] Narration:** And still, one person ends up holding it all together.


## S02  Every question, one person  (0:14.0–0:28.0)

**Statement:** Six questions. / One phone. / Before nine.

**[0:20.6] Narration:** When the answers live in phone calls, spreadsheets and memory, every question comes back to the owner.

**[0:44.4] Narration:** The quote goes through revisions. When the customer approves one, its value is fixed on the job, whatever changes later.


## S05  Order to project · chapter 02  (0:54.6–1:06.4)

**Statement:** Built to the / revision released.

**[1:07.3] Narration:** Engineering releases the general arrangement drawing. Revision two supersedes revision one, and the order is linked to the exact revision it is built from.

**[1:18.7] Narration:** The bill of materials scales to the order, and every line is copied onto it, so a later change never rewrites work already planned.


## S07  Procurement to stores · chapter 04  (1:28.9–1:48.2)

**[1:37.5] Narration:** The purchase order is over the approval limit, so it waits for a decision. Two valves arrive, then the rest, and the hold goes through.

**Statement:** Each stage, / in its order.

**[1:49.1] Narration:** On the floor, each team sees the stages that are ready for them. A stage cannot start until the one before it is done.


## S09  Quality · chapter 05  (1:59.1–2:19.7)

**[2:00.0] Narration:** Checks are measured, with units and limits. The pressure test finds a weeping flange. The skid goes back to assembly, and the failed result stays on record.

**[2:11.2] Narration:** The retest passes. The photograph is taken on a phone, and it belongs to this order, and no other.
