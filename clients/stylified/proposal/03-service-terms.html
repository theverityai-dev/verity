The terms, in plain language

These are the working terms for the hosted Verity service described in document 1. They are written to be read by a business owner. The wording is reviewed by a lawyer before it is signed, and any number in it can be discussed.

01The service and the term

  • What you get. A Verity workspace for Stylified, hosted by PlotArmour, configured to the MVP scope in document 2, for up to 10 named users and one unit. More users or units, and any new module, are priced separately.
  • Start. The service starts on the go-live date, when Stylified accepts the MVP against the criteria in document 2, section 10.
  • Initial term. The service begins with an initial term of 3 months from go-live, billed monthly in advance, which gives us time to tune the workspace to how Stylified works.
  • After the initial term. The service continues month to month. Either side may end it by giving 30 days' written notice (email is enough); the last invoice covers the notice period.

03Hosting and availability

  • The workspace runs on Verity's hosted platform in an Indian data-centre region, with its own isolated data inside a shared, secured system. No other customer can read Stylified's records.
  • We aim for the service to be available at least 99.5% of each month, not counting planned maintenance announced at least 24 hours ahead and problems with Stylified's own network or devices. This is a target we work to, not a guarantee, and it carries no automatic refund.
  • Security fixes and platform improvements are applied by us as part of the monthly fee.
  • Stylified pays no separate hosting, database or storage bills. They are covered by the server line of the monthly fee.

Moving to Stylified's own accounts (an option, not part of the MVP)

  • Stylified may ask to move its workspace to its own Vercel and Supabase accounts, in Stylified's name. We offer this once we have validated that way of running Verity, and quote the move separately. We do not promise a date, and we will not move a live workspace until a rehearsal on a test copy has passed.
  • While the accounts are billed to PlotArmour, the provider charges stay inside the ₹4,500 server line and the ₹15,000 monthly fee does not change. If provider charges ever rise beyond what that line can carry, we will tell Stylified in writing with 60 days' notice before any change to the fee.
  • Stylified is the account owner. It can see everything in those accounts and can revoke our access at any time; our access is recorded and limited to supporting the service. If access is revoked, we cannot support the workspace until it is restored, and the monthly fee continues for the initial term.
  • Each provider's own terms apply to the accounts, including any limit on commercial use of its plans; we will choose plans that allow Stylified's business use.
  • The move needs a short, announced downtime and a data check before and after. The old copy is kept for 30 days and then deleted.

05Backups and restore

  • We take a backup of Stylified's database and files every day and keep them for at least 30 days.
  • Before go-live we rehearse a restore on Stylified's workspace and show you the result. It is one of the acceptance criteria in document 2.
  • If data is lost or damaged, we restore from the latest good backup on request, normally within 2 working days. A restore returns the workspace to the time of the backup, so entries made after it must be entered again.
  • A correction to a wrong entry never needs a restore: entries are corrected by reversal and both rows stay.

06Your data

  • It is yours. Everything Stylified enters (lots, bundles, karigars, wages, photographs, notes) belongs to Stylified. We use it only to run the service for you and to support you.
  • Export at any time. On request we give you all records as CSV and all files in their original form with an index, within 5 working days, at no extra charge.
  • When the service ends. We keep your data for 30 days so you can export it, then delete it from the live system and, on your request, confirm the deletion in writing. Backups age out within a further 30 days.
  • Support access. Our staff open your workspace only to support you; each such session is recorded in your own audit trail and time-limited.

07Security

  • Each company's data is separated by the database itself, not only by the application, and the platform refuses to run if that separation is switched off.
  • Every action is checked against the user's role on the server. Every entry records who made it and when. Entries cannot be edited, only reversed.
  • Traffic is encrypted. Passwords are held by the sign-in system, not by Verity. Secrets are kept in the hosting environment, never in code.
  • If we confirm that Stylified's data has been exposed to someone who should not see it, we tell you without undue delay and within 72 hours of confirming it.

08Ownership of the software

  • Verity, and everything built inside it for this project, remains the property of PlotArmour. Stylified receives the right to use it during the term, for its own business.
  • General parts we build (for example a better supervisor screen) may be reused for other customers. Stylified's data, its rates, its rules and anything marked confidential are never shared.
  • If Stylified later wants its own copy of the code, that is a separate agreement and a separate price.

09Changes and what is out of scope

  • The MVP scope is fixed by document 2. Anything else (a new screen, a new report layout, an integration, a Phase 2 item) needs a written change request with a price and a date, approved by Stylified before work starts.
  • Delays caused by late decisions, late data or late testing from Stylified move our dates by the same time.

10Confidentiality

Each side keeps the other's non-public information confidential, uses it only for this service, and returns or deletes it at the end. We are happy to sign a short mutual confidentiality agreement before Stylified shares its price lists, rates or customer details.

11Liability and law

  • We take care to run the service well, but we cannot promise that it is free of every fault. Our total liability for any claim under this service is limited to the monthly fees Stylified paid in the 3 months before the claim.
  • Neither side is liable for indirect loss, such as lost profit, or for events outside its control.
  • These terms are governed by the laws of India, and the courts at Delhi have jurisdiction. Both sides try first to settle any dispute by talking to each other.
