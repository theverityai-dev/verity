01What we are offering

Verity is PlotArmour's own operations platform. For Stylified we configure a hosted workspace on it for the piece-rate cycle your requirement sheet describes: a lot is cut into numbered bundles, each bundle moves through the stages (pasting, collar and cuff, stitching, button and kaj, finishing and press), each stage is given to a karigar or an outside unit, and what comes back is counted as good, rejected, rework or short. Wages follow from the wage basis agreed for each karigar, and the owner sees all of it on one screen.

The workspace is yours alone: your data is isolated from every other Verity customer, every entry shows who made it and when, and mistakes are corrected by reversal, never by editing history.

03What is included in the MVP

The full scope, screen by screen, is in document 2. In short, the MVP gives each person the screens their job needs:

WhoWhat they get
OwnerFactory dashboard (where every piece is, what is late, what is owed), lots and bundles, karigar accounts, all reports, approvals overview
ManagerEverything the owner sees except wage rates; approves deductions, shortage write-offs and lot hold or close within limits the owner sets
Supervisor newA phone-first floor view for their stages: what is out, what is due, receive and inspect returns with a photo, reissue rework, raise shortages, close the day

Also included: bilingual (Hindi and English) slips and entry labels, a lot cover photograph and defect photographs on rejected or rework entries, in-app alerts for late returns, a reset-able demonstration with fictional data, and a half-day training session.
