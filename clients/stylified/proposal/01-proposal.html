Honest numbers, by design
Good, rejected, rework and short are always kept apart. Every figure traces back to the entry that made it, and a mistake is corrected by reversal, never by rewriting history.

02What is in your workspace

Lots and bundles

Plan a lot, cut numbered bundles (B-0001 onward, no gaps), and print a slip with the bundle number, net pieces, lot and a QR. Lots move through draft, active, on hold, completed and closed.

Issue, take back, rework

Give a bundle to a karigar or an outside unit with a due date. Take it back split into good, rejected, rework and short, with a live "accounted for 96 of 98" line. Rework goes back out and is counted on its own.

Wages and accounts

Wage basis per karigar and stage (issued, returned or accepted good). Advances, recoveries, deductions with a reason, and payments with mode and reference. Every karigar has a clear account.

Dashboards and reports

A factory dashboard for the owner, a floor board by stage, and eight reports that download for Excel: lot status, where each bundle is, work in hand, wages, quality and more.

Photographs

Lot pictures on every card, set up with you, and defect photographs taken on the phone and attached to rejected or rework entries, so a conversation about quality starts from the picture.

Hindi and English

Slips, the munshi's daily entry and every supervisor screen in both languages, chosen on each phone and remembered. Dark and light themes for the cutting room and the evening shift.
