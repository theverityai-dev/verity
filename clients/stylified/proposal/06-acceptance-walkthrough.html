How we agree it is done

Run these checks with us on Stylified's own workspace, one at a time. Each says what to do, what you should see, and leaves a box to tick. When every box is ticked, the owner signs at the end and the service starts.

01Before you start

  • One person from Stylified runs the screens (the owner or Ayushmaan), one from PlotArmour watches and fixes anything we find.
  • Use a test lot with a few bundles, not a live one, until check 17.
  • Have a phone with a camera and a printer connected, so the labels, slips and challans are really printed.
  • A check that fails is written down with what happened, fixed, and run again. It is never skipped.

02The checks

#What to doWhat you should seeResult
1Roles. Sign in as each person: owner, manager, supervisor, munshi, store, cutting master. Try to open a screen the role should not have.Each opens on their own page and sees only their screens. A supervisor cannot open wages. A supervisor cannot give out at a stage that is not theirs, nor to a karigar set under another supervisor.Pass  Fail
2The sheet's reconciliation. Issue 100 pieces on a bundle. Take back 94 good, 2 rejected, 1 rework, 2 short.The screen reads "accounted for 99 of 100, 1 stays open". 100 = 94 + 2 + 1 + 2 + 1 open.Pass  Fail
3Receiver's count. Give out 100. Take back 98 and say 2 are short.2 appear as a case on the worker who held them, with a charge request, both people recorded, the challan and the history kept. The receiver's count is the one that stands.Pass  Fail
4Over-count and correction. Try to take back more pieces than are out. Then reverse a saved entry with a reason.The over-count is refused with the figures and Save stays off. The reversal leaves both rows on record and the figures return to what they were.Pass  Fail
5Photograph. On a rejected or rework return, take a photograph with the phone.It shows on the entry, in the register and in the manager's approval.Pass  Fail
6Limits. Raise a deduction above the manager's limit and one below it.The manager can approve the one below and sees "needs the owner" on the other; only the owner can approve it. Nobody can decide their own request.Pass  Fail
7Closed lot. Complete and close a lot, then try to change it.A closed lot cannot be changed. Closing is refused while pieces are pending or in rework.Pass  Fail
8Fabric. Receive three rolls and print the labels. Scan one with the phone. Cut a lot from them. Enter the same invoice number again.Each roll has a number and a QR label; scanning opens it. The cut lowers each roll's balance by what was used. The repeated invoice is warned before it is saved.Pass  Fail
9Lot cost. Add a thread cost to a lot. Try to add a cost called "Fabric" or "Stitching".The lot's cost equals fabric plus wages plus other costs, less charges recovered, with the cost per good piece. The "Fabric" and "Stitching" costs are refused because they are already counted.Pass  Fail
10Opening balances. Enter opening fabric stock, one karigar's opening balance owed, and an advance already given. Reverse one of them.They appear on the fabric ledger and the karigar's account; the reversal leaves both rows; balances are right.Pass  Fail
11Team. Add a person with a role, stages and a username. Sign in as them. Then switch them off.The new person signs in with the role and stages given. After switching off they cannot sign in, and their history stays.Pass  Fail
12Excel and PDF. Download three reports to Excel and print one to PDF.The totals in the files equal the totals on the screen.Pass  Fail
13Phone and themes. Open every screen of your role at desktop width and at phone width, in light and dark.Nothing is cut off, overlaps or scrolls sideways; every button can be pressed with a thumb.Pass  Fail
14Hindi. Switch a supervisor's phone to Hindi and open every floor screen, the daily entry and a slip.Labels are in Hindi; names and codes stay as they are. (The owner and office screens are English in this release: decision D-15.)Pass  Fail
15Backup. We take a backup and restore it into a separate copy in front of you.The restored copy has the same lots, bundles and balances. A restore returns the workspace to the time of the backup.Pass  Fail
16Ready stock. Enter opening ready stock. Take some out as sold. Try to take out more than is in stock. Correct a count with a reason. Reverse the stock-out.The balance is what was made plus opening less what went out. Taking out more than is there is refused. The count correction needs a reason. The reversal returns the stock and both rows stay on the ledger.Pass  Fail
17Optional stages. Try to skip a required stage on a lot's rate card. Mark a stage optional and skip it.A required stage cannot be skipped, and the screen says why. After marking it optional the lot skips it and its pieces go on to the next stage.Pass  Fail
