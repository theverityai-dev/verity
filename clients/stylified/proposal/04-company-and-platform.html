02Why Verity suits Stylified

Verity is a modular platform: the same foundation (data isolation per company, role-based permissions, audit trail, append-only ledgers, approvals, notifications) carries every module, so new roles, stages and users are added without redeveloping it. Stylified's needs are a piece-rate cycle with careful counting and honest money, which is what the piece-rate module on that foundation does.

05How a Verity workspace feels

One system, every role

The owner, the manager, the supervisor and the munshi use the same product with different screens. What each can do comes from their role, checked on the server.

Honest numbers

Good, rejected, rework and short are kept apart and never mixed. Every figure on a dashboard is worked out from the entries behind it, so a number can always be traced to the entry that made it.

Made for a phone on the floor

Supervisor screens are designed for a phone first: large touch targets, counting with plus and minus, a photograph from the camera.

Dark and light

Every screen works in a dark and a light theme, so it is comfortable in a bright cutting room and on an evening shift.
