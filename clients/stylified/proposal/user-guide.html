Owner

Sees everything, decides what is above the manager's limit, and sets the factory up.

Opens on Factory dashboardLaptop & phone
  1. 1
    Open TodayGiven out, taken back, good, short, what is late, how many requests wait for a decision. Pick any earlier day from the date box.
  2. 2
    Decide what is waitingApprovals shows each request with who asked and why. Anything above the manager's limit says Needs the owner. Approve or decline.
  3. 3
    Read the moneyLot reports and each lot's cost panel: fabric, wages, other costs, cost per good piece. Download Excel or print to PDF.
  4. 4
    Look after peopleTeam: add a person in one sheet with role, stages, language and a temporary password. Reset a password, set who is away, switch someone off.
  5. 5
    Set up once, then adjustSet up the factory walks you from the challan heading to the first lot. Production settings hold stages, reasons, cost categories and the manager's limits.

Can

  • Add, change and switch off people and roles
  • Approve any charge, write-off, hold or lot close
  • Set rates, manager limits, stages and settings
  • Record every payment, advance and recovery
  • See every report, the cost of every lot and the change history
  • Join lots so one carries part of the other's cost

Cannot, by design

  • Edit or delete a posted entry. It is reversed, with a reason, and both rows stay
  • Change a closed lot. Extra work goes on a new lot
  • Decide a request they asked for themselves

The change history lists who did what and when, newest first. Open it before you ask anyone "who changed this?".

Manager

Runs lots, fabric, karigars and payments day to day, and approves within the limits the owner sets.

Opens on Factory dashboardLaptop & phone
  1. 1
    Start with Today and the dashboardLate bundles, open cases, pieces held by each karigar, and the lots in work.
  2. 2
    Clear ApprovalsApprove or decline within the limit (for example ₹1,000 or 5 pieces). Above it the button is greyed and says Needs the owner.
  3. 3
    Run the lotsSet a lot's rate card, start, hold or complete it, cut it, and print its slips. Close a lot within your limit.
  4. 4
    Keep karigars paidRecord advances, recoveries and payments. Every karigar has a clear account with the opening balance.
  5. 5
    Watch the costAdd thread, buttons or packing to a lot, or split one bill across lots by pieces cut. Join two lots when they are finished as one.

Can

  • Everything on lots, bundles, fabric and karigars
  • Give out, take back and reverse any entry with a reason
  • Record wages, advances and payments
  • Take stock out of ready stock and correct a count
  • See lot costing, every report and the change history

Cannot

  • Approve above the limit. It goes to the owner
  • Add people, change roles or change the limits
  • Change production settings (view only)

A decision stays on the case. Who decided, when and what they wrote are kept, so nobody has to remember why a charge was waived.

Munshi

The office clerk. Enters the day once, keeps karigars and their money, and asks for approvals.

Opens on Factory dashboardLaptop · हिंदी or English
  1. 1
    Enter the day in Daily entryChoose Give out or Take back, then bundle, stage, karigar and pieces. Switch to हिंदी at the top if you prefer. The slip and challan print from it.
  2. 2
    Keep Karigars up to dateAdd one, paste a list from Excel, set their supervisor and wage basis, and see each account.
  3. 3
    Record the moneyAdvances, recoveries, payments with mode and reference, and an opening balance for what was owed before Verity.
  4. 4
    Prepare lotsCreate a new lot, set its rate card, cut it from rolls and print the slips. Receive fabric when the store is busy.
  5. 5
    Ask, do not decideA deduction or a write-off is a request. The manager or the owner decides, and you see the answer.

Can

  • Enter give-outs and take-backs for any stage
  • Add karigars and set their rates and supervisor
  • Record advances, recoveries, payments and opening balances
  • Create and cut lots, receive fabric, print slips
  • Reverse their own entry the same day

Cannot

  • Approve a deduction, write-off or close
  • Hold or complete a lot
  • See the change history or production settings
  • Add people or change roles

Entered the wrong count? Press Reverse and say why. The wrong row stays, the reversal sits beside it, and you enter it again correctly.

Supervisor

Works the floor with a phone. Gives out, takes back, and answers for what the karigars hold.

Opens on My floorPhone · हिंदी or English
  1. 1
    Open My floorYour stages, and the pieces out with karigars, waiting to give out, in rework and short. Tap any number to see which bundles.
  2. 2
    Scan the slipTap Scan and point the camera at the bundle's QR. No camera? Type the number.
  3. 3
    Give outPick the karigar, count with + and −, confirm. A challan prints for them to carry.
  4. 4
    Take backCount good, rejected, rework and short. Rejected pieces take a photograph. Short pieces open a case on the karigar who held them.
  5. 5
    Close the dayWrite a short handover note. It is kept for the manager and owner to read.

Can

  • Give out and take back at their own stages, to their own karigars
  • Attach a photograph to rejected or rework pieces
  • Ask for a deduction, a write-off or a lot hold
  • See Who holds what, and the cases on their floor
  • Reverse their own entry on the same day

Cannot

  • See any rate, wage or balance. No money appears anywhere
  • Work at another supervisor's stages, unless covering for them
  • Approve anything or edit anyone's entry

The receiver's count is the truth. Count in front of the karigar. If more comes back than went out, the screen refuses and shows both figures.

Cutting master

Turns fabric rolls into numbered bundles and prints the slips that travel with them.

Opens on LotsPhone & laptop
  1. 1
    Open the lotLots lists what is in work and what is still a draft. Open the lot you are about to cut.
  2. 2
    Cut the lotChoose the rolls used and how many pieces go in each bundle. The metres used come off each roll's balance by themselves.
  3. 3
    Bundles number themselvesB-0001, B-0002 and on, with no gaps. Every bundle carries its own record from now on.
  4. 4
    Print the slipsFull size or a small sticker for the tie. Print only the ones not printed yet, or download a file for a Zebra label printer.
  5. 5
    Move rolls when askedSee the fabric on the shelf and move a roll when a lot needs it.

Can

  • See lots and their bundles
  • Cut a lot and print its slips
  • See fabric rolls and move them

Cannot

  • See money or any karigar rate
  • Give out or take back pieces
  • Set a rate card or change a lot's status

Store

Keeps the fabric shelf and the ready garments. Knows what came in, what is left and what went out.

Opens on FabricPhone · हिंदी or English
  1. 1
    Receive rollsPick the fabric, enter the invoice, supplier, rate and metres. Each roll gets a number and a QR label to stick on it. The same invoice entered twice is warned first.
  2. 2
    Print labelsPrint every roll's label in one go, as an A4 sheet or a Zebra file.
  3. 3
    Count the shelfCount the shelf: scan or type the roll numbers, enter the metres you find, and post. Only the differences are posted, with the reason Stock-take.
  4. 4
    Take garments outReady stock shows what was made. Use Stock out for sold, dispatched or sample. More than is in stock is refused.
  5. 5
    Correct a count, with a reasonFound three extra or two torn? Correct a count needs a reason and keeps both sides on the record.

Can

  • Receive rolls, print labels, move rolls, count the shelf
  • Enter opening ready stock and take stock out
  • Correct a ready-stock count with a reason
  • See lots, bundles and the entries on them

Cannot

  • See wages, balances or karigar rates
  • Give out or take back pieces
  • Change a lot or its rate card

Low fabric is flagged for you. The owner sets one level in settings, and Fabric marks every fabric under it, including ones with nothing left.

The same for everyone

Four things work the same way on every screen, for every role.

Signing in

Use your email, or the username the owner gave you, and your password. People without an email sign in with a username such as ravi.stylified. Forgot it? Ask the owner, who sets a new one on the Team screen.

Hindi or English

The switch sits at the top of the floor, fabric, stock and entry screens. Your choice is remembered on that phone. Names and numbers stay as they are written.

Scanning

Point the camera at a bundle slip, a roll label or a challan and the right screen opens. A hand scanner works as a keyboard, and any number can be typed.

Mistakes

Nothing is edited or deleted. A wrong entry is reversed with a reason; the original, the reversal and the person who made them all stay. Then enter it again correctly.
