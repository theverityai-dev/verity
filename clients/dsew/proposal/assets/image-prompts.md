# DSEW proposal pack: image prompts

Used on the cover and as section banners in the DSEW proposal documents. Shared style line, repeated in every prompt so the
set matches:

> Premium editorial industrial photography, soft diffused light, cool blue colour grade (deep graphite and near-black
> shadows, one accent of clear blue, #0A84FF), shallow depth of field, clean and uncluttered, no text, no logos, no
> watermark, no people's faces.

DSEW builds engineered, one-off electrical equipment (transformers, panels) traced from approved drawing to tested unit.
