A clear position on who owns what.

The position in section 1 is the company's decision. The final wording is reviewed by counsel before it is relied on, and nothing here is a contract until signed.

01The position

DSEW owns its business data

Including everything it enters or uploads. DSEW can take a documented export (records as CSV, files in original form with an index) at handover and once more on request during the testing period.

DSEW receives the deliverables and source code

On full payment: the DSEW-specific code, configuration, templates and documentation produced for this project, handed over in a repository DSEW controls.

PlotArmour keeps its pre-existing assets

Verity's platform, tools, reusable libraries, frameworks and general know-how stay PlotArmour's. DSEW receives a perpetual, non-exclusive licence to use those components within the delivered solution for its own business, at one site, for up to 15 named users, included in the one-time fee. More users or sites are priced separately.

Confidentiality and open source

We do not use DSEW's confidential drawings, prices, specifications or quality formats for anyone else, under a mutual NDA. Open-source components (for example PostgreSQL, Keycloak, Next.js) stay under their own licences, listed in a licence inventory at handover. No paid third-party component is needed.
