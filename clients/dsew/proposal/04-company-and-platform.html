PlotArmour owns and develops Verity, an operations platform for service and manufacturing organisations. Verity is the platform proposed for DSEW.

02Why Verity suits DSEW, and how much is customised

Verity is a modular platform: the same foundation (data isolation per company, role-based permissions, audit trail, stock ledger, approvals, manufacturing orders, project jobs) carries every module, so new departments, product families and users are added without redeveloping it. DSEW's needs here are engineered, one-off products traced from approved drawing to tested unit, which is what the project configures and extends.
