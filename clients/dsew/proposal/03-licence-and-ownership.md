## 1. The position

- **DSEW owns its business data**, including everything it enters or uploads, and can take a documented export (records
  as CSV, files in original form with an index) at handover and once more on request during the 12-month period.
- **On full payment, DSEW receives the agreed deliverables and their source code:** the DSEW-specific code,
  configuration, templates and documentation produced for this project, handed over in a repository DSEW controls.
- **PlotArmour keeps its pre-existing assets:** Verity's platform, tools, reusable libraries, frameworks and general
  know-how stay PlotArmour's. **DSEW receives a perpetual, non-exclusive licence to use those components within the
  delivered solution** for its own business, at one site, for up to 15 named users, included in the one-time fee. More
  users or sites are priced separately.
- We do not use DSEW's confidential drawings, prices, specifications or quality formats for anyone else, under a mutual
  NDA.
- Open-source components used (for example PostgreSQL, Keycloak, Next.js) stay under their own licences, listed in a
  licence inventory at handover. No paid third-party component is needed.
