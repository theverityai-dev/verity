administrators and end users, documentation, deployment on DSEW's premises, warranty.

## 11. Why this price is fair

A comparable package from a large integrator or an ERP partner typically combines a licence per user or per module,
a customisation team charged at larger-firm rates, and an annual support fee, and it still leaves the DSEW-specific
engineering and quality work as paid customisation. You pay for the DSEW-specific work once, with no per-user fee,
and the cost of keeping the system current is visible in the five-year table above.
