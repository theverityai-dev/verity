# Master PRD

## Commercial
- FR-CRM-001 Lead capture with source, owner, value, urgency and notes.
- FR-CRM-002 Lead qualification/conversion preserves history.
- FR-CRM-003 Opportunity pipeline with stage, amount, probability and next action.
- FR-SAL-001 Create quotation from opportunity/customer.
- FR-SAL-002 Quote lines support products/services, quantities, rates, discount/tax fields where configured.
- FR-SAL-003 Accept/reject/expire/cancel quote.
- FR-SAL-004 Convert accepted quote to sales order.
- FR-SAL-005 Track fulfillment per order/line.

## Supply chain
- FR-PUR-001 Create purchase requisition from demand.
- FR-PUR-002 Approve requisition and create purchase order.
- FR-PUR-003 Receive purchase order partially or fully.
- FR-INV-001 Maintain warehouse/site/location hierarchy.
- FR-INV-002 Record receipts, transfers, reservation, issue and adjustments.
- FR-INV-003 Show stock availability and reserved quantity.

## Operations
- FR-OPS-001 Create work order from sold work or internal demand.
- FR-OPS-002 Assign work to site, team and worker.
- FR-OPS-003 Support scheduled/in-progress/blocked/completed states.
- FR-OPS-004 Worker sees today's assignments on mobile/PWA.
- FR-OPS-005 Worker completes checklist, notes and evidence.

## Quality
- FR-QUA-001 Inspection templates and checklists.
- FR-QUA-002 Pass/fail/conditional outcome.
- FR-QUA-003 Failed inspection creates exception/corrective action.
- FR-QUA-004 Track owner, due date, severity, resolution and verification.

## Finance-lite
- FR-FIN-001 Produce invoice-facing record from eligible commercial/operational completion.
- FR-FIN-002 Track issued/partially-paid/paid/overdue status.
- FR-FIN-003 Link revenue/cost/margin views to canonical records.

## Reporting
- FR-RPT-001 Role-aware dashboards.
- FR-RPT-002 KPI → list → record → evidence/audit drill-down.
- FR-RPT-003 Filters by date, region, site, customer, contractor and status.
- FR-RPT-004 Supported export.

## NFR
- NFR-001 Tenant context is explicit for tenant-owned data.
- NFR-002 Material writes occur through approved command/scheduled paths.
- NFR-003 Material transitions are auditable.
- NFR-004 Dedicated single-tenant deployment works without disabling RLS/authorization.
- NFR-005 HQ desktop and field mobile experiences are first-class.
